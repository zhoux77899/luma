# Launcher chrome is Logo plus civil time, not a self-card

Launcher is the Home role, not a card in its own grid. It uses Header-only chrome: a 20 x 20 Logo and 24-hour civil time, with no title, page indicator, Footer, or KeyHint. v0.1 registers Settings, About, and Notes without letter shortcuts; directional selection and Confirm open those Apps, and Back returns to Launcher.
