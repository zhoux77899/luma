# Header glyph and history bars share Battery band colors

Cardputer ADV cannot show Charging, so Wakatake-as-charging no longer carries meaning. The Header battery glyph and Settings Battery history bars therefore share one Battery band: 0–20 Benihi, 21–30 Yamabuki, 31–100 Wakatake. The six-level glyph fill stays a separate quantization; the Settings percent and voltage text stay primary and secondary text. There is no legend.
