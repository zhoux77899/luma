# SETTINGS

Settings is the App that edits device preferences. Open it from [Launcher](launcher.md).

The screen is a Category pane on the left and a Detail pane on the right. Up and Down move inside the focused pane. Confirm moves into the Detail pane, or into a nested editor. Esc returns to the previous pane. Esc from the Category pane returns to Launcher.

The Footer on this split is `Ent ok` and `Esc back`.

The six Settings categories:

- [Display](settings/display.md): Brightness and Theme
- [Sound](settings/sound.md): Volume
- [Network](settings/network.md): Wi-Fi Status, Saved, and Scan
- [Time](settings/time.md): Time zone
- [Battery](settings/battery.md): charge and one-hour history
- [System](settings/system.md): About

Changes to Brightness, Volume, and Theme apply at once. LUMA writes them when you leave Settings.
