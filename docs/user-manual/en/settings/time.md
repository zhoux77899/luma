# Time

Time is a Settings category for civil-time preferences. It is not the Clock service.

Open [Settings](../settings.md), select Time, then Confirm on Time zone.

![Time](../../assets/luma-settings-time.png)

The Time zone row shows the current IANA id, such as `Asia/Shanghai`.

## Time zone

![Time zone](../../assets/luma-settings-time-timezone.png)

The nested split lists Time zone sections on the left: UTC, America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. Confirm into the right list, then Confirm on a zone to apply it.

Each zone shows its id and a UTC offset label.

The Clock uses this selection for civil time in the Header. Time stays blank until the Clock has synchronized.

Footer: `Ent ok`, `Esc back`.
