# Sound

Sound is a Settings category. Open [Settings](../settings.md), select Sound, then Confirm into the Detail pane.

![Sound](../../assets/luma-settings-sound.png)

## Volume

Volume is the 0 to 100 UI audio level. 0 silences UI sound. Left and Right change it by 10. The `-` / `_` and `=` / `+` keys do the same. The bar updates immediately. A change also plays a click at the new level, unless Volume is 0.

Footer: `Ent ok`, `Esc back`.
